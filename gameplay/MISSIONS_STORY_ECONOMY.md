# Missions, Story and Economy

## Story structure
- Main story chapters unlock districts, facilities and Shadow Cave upgrades.
- Side missions are generated from jobs, NPC relationships, exploration and emergencies.
- Free-roam remains available at all times.

## Economy
- Player starts with basic funds and earns money through jobs, contracts, trading and story rewards.
- Costs include food, transport, repairs, property and upgrades.
- NPCs and businesses have simple supply/demand states.

## Mission format
Each mission defines: title, prerequisites, start location, objectives, optional objectives, fail conditions, rewards and world-state changes.

## Dynamic events
Traffic incidents, deliveries, lost NPCs, service calls, races, weather events and discoveries can create optional activities without blocking the main story.
