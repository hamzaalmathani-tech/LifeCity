# Vehicles and Aircraft

## Road vehicles
- Compact, sedan, SUV, sports, off-road, taxi, bus, truck, emergency and utility categories.
- Vehicle ownership, fuel/energy, condition, customization and storage.

## Aviation
- Civil airport: passenger, cargo and ground-service simulation.
- Private airfield: light aircraft and helicopters.
- Military airbase: fictional military aviation assets, restricted access and scripted training/story activities.
- Aircraft systems: throttle, steering, brakes, landing gear, camera modes, takeoff/landing states and damage.

## Water
Boats and service craft are planned for coastal/river districts.

## Performance
Vehicles use level-of-detail and district streaming so distant traffic is simulated cheaply while nearby vehicles receive full physics.
