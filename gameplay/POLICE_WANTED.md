# Police & Wanted System

## Wanted levels
0: normal city life.
1: local response and observation.
2: active pursuit by patrol units.
3: coordinated road response and checkpoints.
4: major city response, helicopter observation and roadblocks.
5: high-alert story response with specialized fictional units.

## AI behavior
Police receive a last-known position, search likely routes, coordinate pursuit units and de-escalate when the player breaks line of sight long enough.

## World integration
Emergency vehicles use traffic rules with priority behavior. Civilians react to dangerous events by fleeing, sheltering or calling services. Wanted state is saved with the player.
