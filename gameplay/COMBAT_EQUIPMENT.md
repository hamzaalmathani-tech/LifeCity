# Combat & Equipment System

## Gameplay-only equipment
LifeCity uses fictional and abstracted equipment for gameplay. Each item has category, rarity, damage class, range class, cooldown, durability and upgrade slots.

## Inventory
- Quick slots for mobile controls.
- Equipment storage at the player's home and Shadow Cave.
- Pickup/drop/swap interface.
- Mission rewards and shop purchases.

## Combat loop
Target selection -> aim -> action -> hit/impact result -> AI reaction -> cooldown/recovery. The system supports non-lethal and fictional effects where appropriate.

## Progression
Equipment unlocks through missions, exploration, shops and the Shadow Cave research tree. No real-world construction or modification instructions are represented.
