# Shadow Cave

A hidden mountain facility discovered through exploration. It is an original game location, not a copy of a copyrighted character or franchise.

## Rooms
- Main garage: vehicle storage and customization.
- Aircraft hangar: helicopter and light-aircraft storage.
- Command center: city map, mission board and surveillance UI.
- Workshop: fictional equipment upgrades and vehicle maintenance.
- Research lab: technology progression and crafting of fictional equipment.
- Training room: movement, driving and combat tutorials.
- Living quarters: rest and save point.
- Secret tunnel: fast-travel entrance to selected world locations after unlock.

## Progression
The cave starts mostly empty. Completing exploration, story and engineering missions unlocks rooms, storage capacity, vehicle slots and fictional equipment.
