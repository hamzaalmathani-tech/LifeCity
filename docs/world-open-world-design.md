# LifeCity — Open World Design v0.1

## Core vision
A seamless open-world life simulation for mobile: one connected city and surrounding wilderness, with optional interiors and streamed districts.

## Major regions
- Downtown: offices, shops, apartments, traffic, nightlife.
- Residential districts: houses, schools, parks, local services.
- Industrial district: warehouses, workshops, logistics.
- Civil airport: terminals, taxiways, hangars, commercial aviation gameplay.
- Private airfield: light aircraft, helicopters, private hangars.
- Military base: restricted area, training facilities, transport aircraft and fictional military aviation.
- Mountain wilderness: caves, trails and hidden facilities.
- Shadow Cave: secret player-owned technology base with garage, workshop, command center, research lab and hangars.

## Player systems
- On-foot movement and mobile controls.
- Vehicles and aircraft with enter/exit interactions.
- Health, energy, hunger, money and reputation.
- Inventory and equipment.
- Missions plus free exploration.
- Save/load.

## NPC simulation
Every important NPC has identity, job, schedule, needs, goals, relationships and memory. NPCs choose actions from available world activities instead of following only scripted paths.

## Transportation
Civilian cars, taxis, buses, trucks, emergency vehicles, motorcycles, boats, helicopters, light aircraft and commercial aircraft. Military vehicles/aircraft are represented as fictional game assets.

## Safety/design boundary
Weapons are fictional game equipment and are implemented as gameplay systems (inventory, aiming, damage, cooldowns, AI reactions) rather than real-world construction instructions.

## Streaming target
Design for district streaming so the mobile build can scale toward a ~1 GB target without requiring the entire world to remain loaded at once.

## First playable milestone
1. Connected city map.
2. Player movement + touch controls.
3. Basic vehicle enter/exit and driving.
4. NPC pedestrian/traffic simulation.
5. Civil airport and private airfield landmarks.
6. Mountain + discoverable Shadow Cave.
7. Mission system and save system.
